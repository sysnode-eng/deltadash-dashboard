PERCENT ?
Net Change %
• Price move over the window
• Window-Total: (close − start price) ÷ start price
Net Intraday %
• Session moves only (open → close) — the part of Net Change not from gaps
• Window-Total: Net Change % − Net Gap % = Σ(close − open) ÷ start price
Net Gap %
• Overnight moves only — up gaps minus down gaps
• Window-Total: Σ(open − close[1]) ÷ start price
Net Flow %
• Traction-weighted dollar volume, as a percent of starting market cap
   Net Traction: (close − close[1]) ÷ True Range
   ↳ proportion of volume allocated (±) by the net move relative to the True Range
• Window-Total: Σ(price × volume × net traction) ÷ start MCAP
True Range % (Avg)
• True range (incl. gaps) as a percent of its median range-bound price
• Window-Averaged: True Range ÷ TrueMid
Day Range % (Avg)
• Day's range (high − low, no gap), as a percent of the day's midpoint
• Window-Averaged: (high − low) ÷ ((high + low) ÷ 2)
Gap Size % (Avg)
• Gap size (direction ignored), as a percent of previous close
• Window-Averaged: |open − close[1]| ÷ close[1]
Turnover % (Avg)
• Daily dollar volume (price × volume) as a percent of market cap
• Window-Averaged: price × volume ÷ MCAP
LOG (Change, Intraday, Gap, Flow)
• Shows the move as a natural-log return
• Formula: ln(1 + metric)
SCALED (Change, Intraday, Gap, Flow)
• Puts every window on equal footing (1D-equivalent)
• Window-Based: metric ÷ √span

RELATIVE window-avg ?
Change Bias ±
• Signed close-to-close move relative to its typical range
• Window-Averaged: (close − close[1]) ÷ ATR
Intraday Bias ±
• Signed session move (open → close) relative to its typical range
• Window-Averaged: (close − open) ÷ ATR = Change Bias − Gap Bias
Gap Bias ±
• Signed gap relative to its typical range
• Window-Averaged: (open − close[1]) ÷ ATR
Flow Bias ±
• Traction-weighted dollar volume relative to its typical dollar volume
   Net Traction: (close − close[1]) ÷ True Range
   ↳ proportion of volume allocated (±) by the net move relative to the True Range
• Window-Averaged: price × volume × net traction ÷ avg(price × volume)
True Range (Rel)
• True Range relative to its typical range
• Window-Averaged: True Range ÷ ATR
Day Range (Rel)
• Day's range (high − low, no gap) relative to its typical day range
• Window-Averaged: (high − low) ÷ avg(high − low)
Gap Size (Rel)
• Gap relative to its typical gap
• Window-Averaged: gap ÷ avg(gap)
Turnover (Rel)
• Dollar volume relative to its typical dollar volume
• Window-Averaged: price × volume ÷ avg(price × volume)
• 1D metrics are 14-day normalized and scaled

PATH multi-day ?
R² ±
• R² (Trend), signed by that window's own net change direction
• Window-Based: R² × sign(Net Change %)
R² (Trend)
• Price vs straight trendline (1 = perfect trend, 0 = no trend)
• Window-Based: corr(price, time)²
Z-Score
• Where price ended vs its window average, in standard deviations
• Window-Based: (close − mean) ÷ Std Deviation
Std Error %
• How widely price wandered around its average, normalized so any window compares fairly
• Window-Based: Std Deviation ÷ mean ÷ √span
Z-Score (Avg)
• Which side of its average price spent its time on
• Window-Averaged: avg(Z-Score)
RSI
• Balance of up-days vs down-days (50 = even, above = buyers winning)
• Window-Based: avgGain ÷ avgLoss (50 baseline)
Anchored RSI (details…)
• Accumulates up- and down-moves across the anchor span
• Stabilized by the prior span’s final magnitude baseline
• Total-Based:  (Σ up + baseline) ÷ (Σ down + baseline) → RSI
RSI-14 Norm
• RSI normalized to a 14-bar basis (can run past 0–100)
• Window-Based: (RSI − 50) × √(span ÷ 14) + 50
